Research Overview

My work combines physics-guided machine learning, inverse design, nonlinear optics, and programmable laser shaping to advance high-brightness electron and X-ray sources.

Research directions

Light Intelligence, AI for Science

Physical AI, digital twins, deep learning, reinforcement learning, multimodal AI, and agentic AI for scientific discovery.

Ultrafast & Nonlinear Optics

Optical waveform design, nonlinear frequency conversion, hollow-core fibers, and high-field light-matter interaction.

Nanophotonics & Quantum Optics

Metasurfaces, plasmonics, optical trapping, photonic circuits, and quantum interconnects.

Accelerator Physics

Laser shaping and source-level control for high-brightness electron beams and next-generation X-ray sources.
